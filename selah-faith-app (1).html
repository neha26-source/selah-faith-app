"The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters."
Psalm 23:1–2 · KJV
Today's Reflection
The image of green pastures and still waters speaks not of ease, but of trust. A shepherd leads; the sheep do not navigate alone. Today's invitation is not to solve your circumstances — it's to rest in the One who already knows the way forward, before the day grows loud.
Where are you striving today instead of simply following?
